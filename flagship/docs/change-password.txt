Changing or Resetting Your Password (Cloud PC and VPN)

Your Cloud PC password and your VPN password are the same password. If you change one, both change automatically. This password expires every three months, so change it before it expires to avoid being locked out.

There are two ways to change it yourself:

1. From your local laptop: You must be connected to the company network by LAN. Once connected, press Ctrl+Alt+Delete and select "Change a password."

2. From inside the Cloud PC: Look at the top of the screen and press the Ctrl+Alt+Delete icon, then select "Change a password" and follow the prompts.

If your password has already expired and you can no longer change it yourself, call IT to have it reset on your behalf.
