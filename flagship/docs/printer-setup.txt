Requirements:
Before setting up a printer, your laptop must be connected to the office network using a LAN cable. Wireless will not work for printer setup. Most of our machines run Windows 11.

Steps to add a printer by IP address:
1. Open Settings, then go to Bluetooth & devices, then Printers & scanners.
2. Click "Add device".
3. Wait for it to search, then click "Add manually".
4. Choose "Add a printer using an IP address or hostname".
5. Set Device type to "TCP/IP Device".
6. Enter the printer's IP address (for example, x.x.x.20).
7. Click Next and follow the prompts to finish.

Notes:
The printer IP address always ends in 20. If you do not know the full IP, contact IT support.
