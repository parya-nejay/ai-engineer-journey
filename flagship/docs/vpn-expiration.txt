Checking and Renewing Your VPN Access

Your VPN password expires every three months and is the same as your Cloud PC password. To avoid losing access, change it before it expires.

To check your VPN expiration date, go to its.example.com, press VPN, then press VPN Request. Your name will already be listed — find your entry and check the expiration date shown next to it.

To change your password yourself, see "Changing or Resetting Your Password (Cloud PC and VPN)."

If your VPN password has already expired, you can no longer change it yourself. Call IT to have it reset.
