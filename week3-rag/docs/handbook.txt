Acme Corp Employee Handbook
============================

About Acme Corp
---------------
Acme Corp was founded in 2018 by Sarah Chen and David Park.
Sarah Chen is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
David Park is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
Our headquarters is in Toronto, Canada.

Vacation Policy
---------------
Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per year.
Vacation days reset every January 1st.
Unused days do not roll over.

Remote Work
-----------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Friday is a mandatory in-office day for all team meetings.

Health Benefits
---------------
Acme Corp provides full dental, vision, and medical coverage.
Benefits start on day 1 of employment.
